# UI reference pass

Layout and structure only, compared against two reference layouts (a landing page and a four-cell bento). No text, names, logos, colours or art were taken. The dusk palette stays.

| Area | Reference does | Lastbell before | Lastbell now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bento grid | One joined grid, 1px shared lines, square markers at every intersection | Separate gapped cards, markers on each card | Joined grids (`.joined`) with markers at intersections, on How it works, the four rules, stats and Flywheel tiles |
| Bento art | Illustration sits straight in the cell, text pinned at the bottom | Illustration in its own bordered box | Art sits in the cell, text at the bottom |
| Stats | Headline and button on the left, joined stat cells on the right | Four tiles in a row under a centered title | Split layout: headline and "Read the contract" left, joined 2x2 cells right |
| Colour bands | Short two-tone tab at the top-left of a section | Full-width band | Short two-tone tab per section (full three-colour band kept on the footer) |
| Hero | Big headline, call-to-action buttons, dot-matrix art behind | Headline and widget only | Added "How it works" and "See the flywheel" buttons and a faint dot-matrix ridge behind the headline |

Not copied on purpose: testimonial carousel, integrations cloud, newsletter block. They have no meaning for this product.
